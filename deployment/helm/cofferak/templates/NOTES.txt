cofferak is starting and waits for you to initialise the vault.

1. Create the vault. This prints the root private key ONCE: save the whole
   block offline, it is your recovery key.

   kubectl exec -it -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "cofferak.fullname" . }}-0 -- cofferak init --root

2. Enroll this pod as a host:

   kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "cofferak.fullname" . }}-0 -- cofferak init --host {{ include "cofferak.hostId" . }}

3. Give a service its own token (printed once):

   kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "cofferak.fullname" . }}-0 -- cofferak enroll-service myapp --paths myapp --perm rw

The pod becomes Ready after step 2.
